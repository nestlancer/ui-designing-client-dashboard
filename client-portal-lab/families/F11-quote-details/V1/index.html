Work completed to the cancellation date is billable. The deposit is non-refundable once kickoff has happened, because studio time is reserved in advance.
Validity
This price holds until 20 Oct 2026. After that the quote locks and a re-quote is required; scope history and the accepted deposit carry forward.

Full IP transfers on final payment. Until then the studio retains copyright, and may show the work in its portfolio only after launch.
