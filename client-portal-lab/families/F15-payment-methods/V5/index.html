What "verify" means here

Card verification

A ₹1 authorisation is placed and reversed immediately. It proves the card is live without moving money.

UPI collect

You approve a collect request in your UPI app. If it lapses you can resend it — a new request replaces the old one.

Re-authentication

Your bank asks you to confirm again after a decline. No new card number is entered in the portal.

Razorpay

Verification and re-authentication are gateway flows. Manual token entry is not supported, and UPI autopay mandates are demo-only in this lab.
