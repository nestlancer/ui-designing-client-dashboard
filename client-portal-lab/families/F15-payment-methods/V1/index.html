How storage works

Where each kind of method lives.

Saved card

Razorpay stores a token. The portal stores the last four digits, the network and the expiry so you can recognise it.

UPI

A VPA can be saved as a mandate with a per-payment limit. Without one, UPI still works at checkout with a fresh VPA.

Bank transfer

Nothing is stored. You get an account block and enter the UTR, and the studio confirms it.

Removing a method removes the token only. Finance records — invoices, receipts, the ledger — are kept for seven years as required.
