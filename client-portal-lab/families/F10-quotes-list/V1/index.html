How quoting works

The short version, so nothing here surprises you.

You request 5 Oct

Brief, budget and timeline are captured once and reused for every quote.

Studio prices it 5 Oct

Line items, GST, payment schedule and a validity date — 15 days by default.

You sign or ask for changes by 20 Oct

Sign in three steps. Asking for changes keeps the quote alive and creates a revision.

After validity 21 Oct →

The quote locks. Nothing is charged. One click asks for a re-quote at current rates.

Expiry is not a penalty. Seven quotes expired because the work was paused, not because of a missed payment.
