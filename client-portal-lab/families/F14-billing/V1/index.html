How billing works

Three objects, one money trail.

Milestone payment

Tied to a project milestone. Processing until Razorpay confirms, then it settles and unlocks the deliverable.

Invoice

A tax document with its own due date. Payable by card, UPI or bank transfer (with UTR).

Receipt

Generated per settled payment. The ledger above is the index of all three.

Disputes: any invoice can be disputed within 14 days from its page. A dispute pauses late-fee logic, never access to files.
