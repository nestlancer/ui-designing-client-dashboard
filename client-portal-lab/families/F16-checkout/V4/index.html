If something goes wrong

The payment succeeded but nothing changed
Razorpay confirms on its server callback. Refresh once; if the milestone is still locked after two minutes, open the payment status page and use the receipt reference — the callback is retried automatically for 24 hours.
My card was declined
No money moved. The ledger keeps a failed row so you can see the attempt, and the same reference can be retried with UPI or a bank transfer instead.
I sent a bank transfer without the UTR
Verification cannot match it, so the payment stays in processing. Reply in the billing thread with the reference and the studio will attach it manually.
I want a refund
Milestone payments are refundable only through a dispute on the payment record — the dispute form is on the payment status page, and ₹0.00 is currently in dispute on this account.
